# 25 Global Opportunities (2026-27)

Mobile-first dark single-page app listing 25 verified-where-possible opportunities for a 15-year-old programmer in Egypt (programming, CS, logic/philosophy, international travel/exchange). Environmental and climate-focused programs are excluded.

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`: the app (search, filter chips, sort, detail sheet per item)
- `data.json`: the structured data for all 25 items
- Research date: Nov 25, 2026. Any field that could not be confirmed on an official page is marked "Unconfirmed / Verify on Official Page".

Excluded by request: One Teen Story, Non-Trivial, AFS Global STEM Academies, Diamond Challenge, Horizon Academic Essay Prize, Robotics for Good Youth Challenge, MIT App Inventor Global Appathon, Think Essay Prize, The Junior Academy, Blue Planet Awareness Contest, UWC, FLEX/YES.
